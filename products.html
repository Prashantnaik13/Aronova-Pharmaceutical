Gastroenterology

Cetpan-D

Pantoprazole IP 40 mg + Domperidone IP 30 mg Sustained Release Capsule · 10×10 Capsules

"When acid strikes, choose fast, effective relief."

Dual Pellet in Capsule (DPC) technology separates the two actives into distinct pellet populations — so acid suppression and prokinetic action are released where and when they work best, in a single capsule.

Key Actions

  • Reduces gastric acid secretion
  • Supports gastric motility
  • Helps control nausea and vomiting
  • No extrapyramidal effects associated with dopamine-antagonist prokinetics

Indications

GERD Moderate to severe reflux oesophagitis Dyspepsia & bloating Acid aspiration during anaesthesia NSAID-induced gastritis Nocturnal gastric pain with acid-peptic disorders
DPC advantage: uniform drug release · no local irritation · better bioavailability · faster relief.
Respiratory Care

Aro Q-DX

Dextromethorphan HBr 10 mg + Chlorpheniramine Maleate 2 mg + Phenylephrine HCl 5 mg / 5 ml Syrup

"When cough needs attention."

A balanced three-in-one syrup that raises the cough threshold, calms the allergic component and clears congestion — so patients can breathe easy through the day.

How Each Ingredient Works

  • Dextromethorphan HBr (10 mg): centrally acting antitussive that increases the threshold of cough
  • Chlorpheniramine Maleate (2 mg): time-tested antihistamine
  • Phenylephrine HCl (5 mg): powerful decongestant

Indications

Dry cough Nasal & sinus congestion Common cold Allergic rhinitis

Store protected from light at a temperature not exceeding 30 °C. Shake well before use.

Pain & Inflammation

Acovex-SP

Aceclofenac 100 mg + Paracetamol 325 mg + Serratiopeptidase 15 mg Tablets · 10×10 Tablets

"Break the chain of painful inflammatory condition."

Three complementary mechanisms in one tablet — analgesic, antipyretic and anti-oedematic — for acute flare-ups where pain and swelling travel together.

Component Rationale

  • Aceclofenac: shows greater decrease in pain compared to nimesulide in low back pain
  • Paracetamol: lower side-effect profile than NSAIDs
  • Serratiopeptidase: anti-inflammatory, anti-oedemic and fibrinolytic activity; speeds up tissue repair and healing

Indications

Flare-up of OA / RA / Gout Low back pain Pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) Post-traumatic inflammation
Allergy Care

Montaro-LC

Montelukast 10 mg + Levocetirizine Hydrochloride 5 mg Tablets

"Allergy doesn't follow a season."

Dual-pathway control that blocks both leukotriene and H1 receptors — addressing the nasal symptoms of allergy while supporting patients whose allergy overlaps with airway involvement.

Clinical Benefits

  • Inhibits leukotriene and H1 receptors
  • Helps relieve distressing allergy symptoms
  • Improves nasal symptoms
  • Provides control of allergy symptoms in patients with airway involvement
  • May help reduce bronchoconstriction in appropriate patients

Indications

Seasonal allergic rhinitis Perennial allergic rhinitis Rhinitis associated with asthma
