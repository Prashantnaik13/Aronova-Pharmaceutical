About Aronova

A pharmaceutical company built on a simple, non-negotiable idea — that every medicine leaving our warehouse should be safe, effective and honestly documented.

Who We Are

Safe Medicines. Better Lives.

Aronova Pharmaceutical Private Limited is a Pune-based pharmaceutical company engaged in marketing and supply of quality-assured formulations. Our registered office sits in the Kurkumbh MIDC belt at Betwadi, Taluka Daund, with a dedicated warehouse and dispatch office at Saswad, Taluka Purandar.

We focus on the therapeutic segments that Indian clinicians encounter every single day — acidity and reflux, cough and cold, acute pain and inflammation, and allergy. Rather than spreading thin, we concentrate on a compact portfolio of well-established molecules delivered in modern, patient-friendly dosage forms.

Our promise to partners is equally straightforward: correct documentation, consistent availability and a single point of contact who actually picks up the phone.

Our Mission

To make dependable, quality-assured medicines accessible across India by combining clinically relevant formulations with a supply chain that chemists, distributors and institutions can rely on without chasing.

Our Vision

To be a trusted pharmaceutical partner in every territory we serve — recognised for product quality, transparent compliance and relationships that outlast individual transactions.

What We Stand For

Principles that guide every decision

These are not slogans on a wall — they determine what we list, who we source from and how we respond when something goes wrong.

Quality without compromise

Every batch is sourced against defined specifications and verified on receipt. We would rather delay a dispatch than ship something we would not give our own families.

Compliance as a baseline, not a badge

Three valid drug licence numbers, GST registration and full corporate identification are maintained and shared openly with every partner.

Relevance over range

We grow our portfolio deliberately, adding a product only when it answers a real clinical need and fits the way Indian prescribers actually practise.

Partners, not transaction IDs

Distributors, stockists and chemists get a named point of contact, honest timelines and follow-through on every commitment we make.

Compliance & Credentials

Registered, licensed and traceable

Our Journey

From incorporation to a growing portfolio

  • Incorporation Aronova Pharmaceutical Private Limited is incorporated in Pune, Maharashtra (CIN U46497PN2026PTC252245), established to market and supply quality formulations.
  • Licensing The company secures three drug licence numbers — MH-PZ3-635187, MH-PZ3-635188 and MH-PZ3-635189 — covering its pharmaceutical trading and distribution operations.
  • Infrastructure A registered office is established at Betwadi near Kurkumbh MIDC, Taluka Daund, backed by Warehouse No. 1 and a dispatch office at Saswad, Taluka Purandar.
  • Portfolio launch Four brands go to market across gastroenterology, respiratory, pain and allergy care — Cetpan-D, Aro Q-DX, Acovex-SP and Montaro-LC.
  • Growing network The company continues to expand its distributor, stockist and institutional partner network across Maharashtra and neighbouring territories.
