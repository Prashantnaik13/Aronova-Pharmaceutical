Why Aronova

A supply partner you can plan around

We combine dependable sourcing, disciplined documentation and responsive service so that chemists, distributors and institutions never have to chase a shipment.

Licensed & documented

Operations run under three Maharashtra drug licence numbers with full GST, CIN, PAN and TAN registration — every consignment ships with traceable paperwork.

Reliable dispatch

A dedicated dispatch office and warehouse at Saswad keeps orders moving, with clear coordination from order confirmation to delivery.

Quality-assured sourcing

Every batch is sourced against defined specifications and checked on receipt, so that what reaches the patient matches what the label promises.

Trade-friendly packs

Pack configurations such as 10×10 capsules and tablets are designed for fast shelf turnover and simple stock rotation at the chemist counter.

Partner-first approach

We work closely with distributors, stockists and healthcare professionals, supporting them with product literature and prompt response to requirements.

Single-point support

One customer-care number and one email for orders, product queries and distribution enquiries — no bouncing between departments.
